# AI Impact Research

Research on AI from frontier labs and primary sources: usage patterns, capabilities, trajectories, and implications for higher education.

## November 2026 update

**Updated November 1, 2026.** This site now includes evidence published through October 2026. The December 2025 compilation is kept in the archive, with known errors marked.

### How people use AI

- **Reach:** OpenAI says more than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week ([OpenAI, Oct 7, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/), vendor-reported). Alphabet says the Gemini app is at one billion users ([Google, Oct 28, 2026](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q3-2026/), vendor-reported). Pew found 49% of U.S. adults use chatbots, up from 33% in 2024, though the question wording changed ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/), survey).
- **Automation:** The August 2025 automation spike did not hold. Anthropic's November 2025 sample shows augmentation back ahead, 52% to 45% ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report), vendor-reported). Google's own study found end-to-end automation under 10% of non-routine cognitive work conversations ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038), vendor-reported).
- **Global gap:** Microsoft's telemetry puts use at 28.8% of working-age people in the Global North and 16.2% in the Global South, a gap of 12.6 points, up from 10.6 in H2 2025 ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf), vendor-reported).
- **Gender:** Pew finds the gap in ever-use has closed (50% of men, 47% of women), but men use chatbots daily more often, 27% to 20% ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/the-gender-gap-in-ai/), survey).

### What AI can do

- **Benchmarks change fast.** OpenAI stopped reporting SWE-bench Verified after finding flaws in 59.4% of 138 audited problems that o3 did not consistently solve ([OpenAI, Feb 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/), vendor-reported). Epoch AI addressed errors in 42% of FrontierMath problems in June 2026 ([Epoch AI, Jun 12, 2026](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath), third-party measurement).
- **Long tasks remain hard.** The best agent listed on the OSWorld 2.0 page, Claude Opus 4.8, completes 20.6% of long-horizon computer tasks ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/), third-party measurement). The top Remote Labor Index score is 21.25% of freelance projects ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli), third-party measurement).
- **Vendors disagree about the same model.** Google lists GPT-6 Astra at 34.2% on Agent's Last Exam, while OpenAI lists 59.3% on a benchmark of the same name ([Google DeepMind, Oct 13, 2026](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/); [OpenAI, GPT-6 Astra page, undated](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/), vendor-reported).
- **Learning evidence conflicts.** A randomized trial of a course-integrated AI tutor with 2,379 students reports grade losses of 0.27 to 0.37 standard deviations, clearest among sections of the same course. The authors say the full sample alone does not show significant adverse effects ([IZA Institute of Labor Economics, Oct 2026](https://docs.iza.org/dp18989.pdf), unreviewed paper). A proctored experiment with 211 students found higher test scores with open generative AI access ([arXiv, Jul 9, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08849), unreviewed paper). Both are unreviewed papers.
- **Labor and productivity.** A Federal Reserve governor said there is limited evidence so far that AI is changing the structure of the labor market ([Federal Reserve, Sep 28, 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/cook20260928a.htm), official source).

## How to read this report

Every new finding carries one of these labels, so you can judge how much weight it can carry:

- **Vendor-reported:** a company reporting on its own product or customers.
- **Third-party measurement:** an outside group measuring directly, such as a benchmark owner.
- **Official source:** a government or international institution.
- **Survey:** people reporting on themselves in a survey.
- **Peer-reviewed:** a paper published after journal review.
- **Unreviewed paper:** a working paper or preprint.
- **Expert compilation:** a report that summarizes other people's work.
- **Consultancy estimate:** a model or comparison from a consulting firm.

**[How this was checked](method.md)** gives the method and its limits, including what was left out. **[The corrections log](corrections.md)** lists every error found in the earlier version. **[Report an error](https://github.com/vishalsachdev/ai-impact/issues/new?title=Error%20report&body=Page%3A%0AClaim%3A%0ASource%3A%0AWhat%20is%20wrong%3A)** by opening a public GitHub issue.
