# Research Question 5: Enterprise vs Consumer Usage Patterns

## Primary Question
How do enterprise and consumer AI usage differ in adoption patterns, use cases, pricing models, and privacy requirements?

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI stated on 2026-03-31 that enterprise makes up more than 40% of its revenue and is on track to reach parity with consumer by the end of 2026. ([OpenAI, Mar 31, 2026](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/))
- **Survey.** Menlo Ventures estimated in December 2025 that Anthropic holds 40% of enterprise LLM API market share against 27% for OpenAI. ([Menlo Ventures, Dec 9, 2025](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/))
- **Official source.** The US Census Bureau's BTOS found AI use by US businesses hovered between 17% and 20% from 2025-12-14 to 2026-05-03 (19.8% in the latest period), after the question was reworded in November 2025 to ask about use in any business function. ([U.S. Census Bureau, May 26, 2026](https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html))
- **Third-party measurement.** Ramp's AI Index reported that 55.9% of businesses on Ramp paid for AI in September 2026, with Anthropic at 43.8% and OpenAI at 40.6%. ([Ramp, Oct 14, 2026](https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-oct-2026))
- **Survey.** McKinsey's August 2026 survey (1,719 participants in 97 nations) says nearly nine in ten respondents report regular AI use in at least one business function, and 44 percent say AI is scaling across their enterprise, up from 38 percent a year ago. ([McKinsey, Aug 25, 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai))
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI said on 2026-01-16 that it would test ads in the US for Free and Go tiers while Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscriptions would not include ads, and that conversations stay private from advertisers. ([OpenAI, Jan 16, 2026](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic announced on 2026-02-04 that Claude will remain ad-free. ([Anthropic, Feb 4, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-is-a-space-to-think))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 15 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
