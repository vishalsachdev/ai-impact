# Research Question 3: Demographics & Adoption Trends

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. The November 2026 section is current. Everything after it is the December 2025 compilation, kept as dated history. Superlatives and words like "latest", "leading" or "now" in that older text describe December 2025.*

- Pew found about half of U.S. adults (49%) said in February 2026 that they use AI chatbots such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, up from about a third (33%) in summer 2024. ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/))
- Pew found the gender gap in overall U.S. adult chatbot use has closed: it was 11 percentage points (men more likely) two years earlier; men and women now report 50% and 47% ever use, but men use daily more (27% vs 20%). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/the-gender-gap-in-ai/))
- Pew found 63% of U.S. adults under 50 use AI chatbots versus about four in ten of those 50 to 64 and fewer of those 65 and older, and a third of adults under 50 use them daily (February 2026). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/))
- Gallup's Q2 2026 workforce survey found 52% of U.S. workers use AI in their role, 30% frequently and 15% daily. ([Gallup, Jul 20, 2026](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712736/organizational-adoption-jumps-six-points.aspx))
- The St. Louis Fed's Real-Time Population Survey shows the share of U.S. adults aged 18 to 64 using generative AI rose from 45% (August 2024) to 62% (May 2026), and the authors say age, education and sex explain little of why some workers use AI for more tasks than others. ([St. Louis Fed, Sep 1, 2026](https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2026/sep/what-work-does-generative-ai-do))
- Pew's teen survey (fielded 2025-09-25 to 2025-10-09) found 64% of U.S. teens use AI chatbots and about three in ten use them daily; Black teens (35%) and Hispanic teens (33%) use them daily more than White teens (22%). ([Pew Research Center, Dec 9, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 16 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.

## Primary Question
Who is adopting AI platforms, and how are demographic patterns evolving across age, gender, geography, and socioeconomic factors?

## Key Hypothesis
AI adoption is following different patterns than previous technology waves, with surprising demographic shifts including gender parity achievement and emerging economy leadership.

## Significance
Understanding demographic patterns is critical for:
- Predicting future adoption curves
- Identifying underserved populations
- Educational policy and curriculum design
- Digital divide mitigation strategies
- Market sizing and opportunity assessment

## Key Findings Summary
- **Gender parity achieved**: ChatGPT now 52.4% female (vs male-dominated in 2024) ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: The 52.4% overstates the source. OpenAI reported about 52% among users whose names could be classified, by July 2025. This is inferred from names, not measured gender. Pew's 2026 survey shows the gap in ever-use has closed, but men use chatbots daily more often (see the update above).*
- **Youth adoption**: 64% of U.S. teens use AI chatbots, 28% daily ([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.com/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/))
- **Age concentration**: 53.24% of ChatGPT users are 18-34 years old ([DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/chatgpt-statistics/)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: The 53.24% comes from an aggregator, not a primary source, and is a share of users rather than an adoption rate by age. Use the Pew 2026 age figures in the update above.*
- **Emerging economies leading**: India, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa have highest adoption rates ([Modern Diplomacy](https://moderndiplomacy.eu/2025/03/04/why-is-ai-adoption-slower-in-the-global-south-and-how-can-it-leap-forward/)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: This holds only for respondents in the 14-country OECD-Cisco poll. Microsoft's Q2 2026 telemetry lists South Africa at 23.9%, Mexico at 21.4%, Brazil at 20.2% and India at 18.5%, against 28.8% for the Global North ([Microsoft, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf)).*
- **Racial disparities (U.S. teens)**: Black/Hispanic teens use AI more than white teens ([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.com/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/))
- **Generational divide**: Under-35s far more likely to use and trust AI ([OECD-Cisco](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oecd-cisco-research-finds-stark-geographical-and-generational-divides-in-ai-uptake-and-digital-well-being-302632418.html))
