# Research Question 3: Demographics & Adoption Trends

## Primary Question
Who is adopting AI platforms, and how are demographic patterns evolving across age, gender, geography, and socioeconomic factors?

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Survey.** Pew found about half of U.S. adults (49%) said in February 2026 that they use AI chatbots such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, up from about a third (33%) in 2024, though the 2024 question was asked only of those who had heard at least a little about chatbots. ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/))
- **Survey.** Pew found the gender gap in overall U.S. adult chatbot use has closed: it was 11 percentage points (men more likely) two years earlier; men and women now report 50% and 47% ever use, but men use daily more (27% vs 20%). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/the-gender-gap-in-ai/))
- **Survey.** Pew found 63% of U.S. adults under 50 use AI chatbots versus about four in ten of those 50 to 64 and fewer of those 65 and older, and a third of adults under 50 use them daily (February 2026). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/))
- **Survey.** Gallup's Q2 2026 workforce survey found 52% of U.S. workers use AI in their role, 30% frequently and 15% daily. ([Gallup, Jul 20, 2026](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712736/organizational-adoption-jumps-six-points.aspx))
- **Official source.** The St. Louis Fed's Real-Time Population Survey shows the share of U.S. adults aged 18 to 64 using generative AI rose from 45% (August 2024) to 62% (May 2026), and the authors say age, education and sex explain little of why some workers use AI for more tasks than others. ([St. Louis Fed, Sep 1, 2026](https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2026/sep/what-work-does-generative-ai-do))
- **Survey.** Pew's teen survey (fielded 2025-09-25 to 2025-10-09) found 64% of U.S. teens use AI chatbots and about three in ten use them daily; Black teens (35%) and Hispanic teens (33%) use them daily more than White teens (22%). ([Pew Research Center, Dec 9, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 16 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
