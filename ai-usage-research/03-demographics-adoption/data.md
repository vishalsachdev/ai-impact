# Demographics & Adoption: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Survey.** Gallup's American Job Quality Study found U.S. college graduates were more than twice as likely as workers without a degree to use AI daily or weekly (40% vs 17%), and 37% of managers vs 25% of individual contributors. ([Gallup, Oct 6, 2026](https://news.gallup.com/poll/714602/benefits-work-unevenly-distributed.aspx)) *Method: Fielded 2026-01-26 to 2026-03-24, 15,482 U.S. employees (sample of 17,874 working adults aged 18-75), margin of error plus or minus 1 point.*
- **Official source.** The St. Louis Fed's Real-Time Population Survey shows the share of U.S. adults aged 18 to 64 using generative AI rose from 45% (August 2024) to 62% (May 2026), and the authors say age, education and sex explain little of why some workers use AI for more tasks than others. ([St. Louis Fed, Sep 1, 2026](https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2026/sep/what-work-does-generative-ai-do)) *Method: Real-Time Population Survey, nationally representative, U.S. adults 18-64.*
- **Survey.** Gallup's Q2 2026 workforce survey found 52% of U.S. workers use AI in their role, 30% frequently and 15% daily. ([Gallup, Jul 20, 2026](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712736/organizational-adoption-jumps-six-points.aspx)) *Method: Gallup Panel, employed U.S. adults, fielded 2026-05-06 to 2026-05-20, n=22,573, margin of error plus or minus 0.9 points.*
- **Survey.** Pew found about half of U.S. adults (49%) said in February 2026 that they use AI chatbots such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, up from about a third (33%) in 2024, though the 2024 question was asked only of those who had heard at least a little about chatbots. ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/)) *Method: Pew American Trends Panel, 5,119 U.S. adults, fielded Feb 17-23, 2026. The 2024 question wording differed, so the rise from 33% has a wording break.*
- **Survey.** Pew found 44% of U.S. adults ever use ChatGPT in February 2026, up from 34% in 2025, and 57% of adults under 50 versus 28% of those 50 and older. ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/)) *Method: Pew American Trends Panel Wave 187, 5,119 U.S. adults, fielded 2026-02-17 to 2026-02-23, margin of sampling error plus or minus 1.6 points, response rate 87%.*
- **Survey.** Pew found 63% of U.S. adults under 50 use AI chatbots versus about four in ten of those 50 to 64 and fewer of those 65 and older, and a third of adults under 50 use them daily (February 2026). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/)) *Method: Pew American Trends Panel Wave 187, 5,119 U.S. adults, fielded 2026-02-17 to 2026-02-23, margin of sampling error plus or minus 1.6 points, response rate 87%.*
- **Survey.** Pew found the gender gap in overall U.S. adult chatbot use has closed: it was 11 percentage points (men more likely) two years earlier; men and women now report 50% and 47% ever use, but men use daily more (27% vs 20%). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/the-gender-gap-in-ai/)) *Method: Pew American Trends Panel Wave 187, 5,119 U.S. adults, fielded 2026-02-17 to 2026-02-23, margin of sampling error plus or minus 1.6 points, response rate 87%.*
- **Survey.** Pew found 70% of Asian American adults use AI chatbots versus roughly half or fewer of Hispanic, Black and White adults, and roughly half of Asian adults use them daily (February 2026). ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/racial-and-ethnic-differences-in-how-adults-use-and-view-ai/)) *Method: Pew American Trends Panel Wave 187, 5,119 U.S. adults, fielded 2026-02-17 to 2026-02-23, margin of sampling error plus or minus 1.6 points, response rate 87%.*
- **Survey.** The Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2026 found 17% of the youngest age group use AI chatbots for news weekly versus 5% of the oldest, and 10% globally, up from 7% a year earlier. ([Reuters Institute, Jun 16, 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/emerging-uses-ai-chatbots-news-and-what-it-means-journalism)) *Method: Online survey across about 48 markets (45 markets for some items), weekly use of AI chatbots for news.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reported in May 2026 that users with typically feminine names represented a growing share of ChatGPT usage on consumer plans. OpenAI infers this from names; it does not measure gender. ([OpenAI, May 11, 2026](https://openai.com/signals/research/2026q1-update/)) *Method: Vendor-reported.*
- **Survey.** Pew's February 2026 follow-up on the same teen survey found about half of parents say their teen uses chatbots while 64% of teens say they do, so parent reports understate teen use. ([Pew Research Center, Feb 24, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/how-teens-use-and-view-ai/)) *Method: Same Pew teen survey as the December 2025 report (1,458 teens, fielded 2025-09-25 to 2025-10-09).*
- **Official source.** OECD data for 2025 or the latest year show more than one third of individuals across the OECD used generative AI tools, with a 53.6 point age gap, about 21 point gaps by education and by income, and a 4.2 point gender gap. ([OECD, Jan 28, 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/announcements/2026/01/ai-use-by-individuals-surges-across-the-oecd-as-adoption-by-firms-continues-to-expand)) *Method: Official statistics, OECD countries where data exist, use in the last three months, 2025 or latest year.*
- **Unreviewed paper.** A UK preprint using two waves (2023-2024) of the UK Public Attitudes to Data and AI Tracker (N=9,172) finds men report substantially higher levels of frequent personal GenAI use than women. ([arXiv, Jan 7, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03880)) *Method: Preprint by the authors, UK Public Attitudes to Data and AI Tracker, two waves 2023-2024, 'frequent personal use', not 'ever use'.*
- **Survey.** Pew's teen survey (fielded 2025-09-25 to 2025-10-09) found 64% of U.S. teens use AI chatbots and about three in ten use them daily; Black teens (35%) and Hispanic teens (33%) use them daily more than White teens (22%). ([Pew Research Center, Dec 9, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/)) *Method: 1,458 U.S. teens 13-17 surveyed online via Ipsos, 2025-09-25 to 2025-10-09, margin of error plus or minus 3.3 points.*
- **Survey.** The OECD's own December 2025 brief on its Cisco poll (about 1,000 respondents per country in 14 countries, early 2025) says generative AI adoption is highest among 18-35 year olds with India, Brazil and South Africa leading and Germany, France and Italy lower; the brief warns the results need caution because of the poll's nature. ([OECD, Dec 4, 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-do-people-experience-new-technologies-and-generative-ai_49b8d10e-en/full-report.html)) *Method: OECD WISE Centre poll with Cisco.*
- **Expert compilation.** Stanford's 2026 AI Index public-opinion chapter says that in 2025, 58% of employees globally reported using AI at work semiregularly or regularly, with the share above 80% in India, China, Nigeria, the UAE, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/public-opinion))
