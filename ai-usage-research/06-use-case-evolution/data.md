# Use Case Evolution Over Time: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's October 2026 release shows that across 74 countries the ten most common work activities fell from 56.0% of classified work messages in Q1 2025 to 51.1% in Q3 2026, so consumer ChatGPT work use became less concentrated. ([OpenAI, Oct 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-expanding-world-of-work/)) *Method: Individual ChatGPT plans only, excludes enterprise and Codex, work-related messages mapped to O-NET IWAs, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that among about 6,200 workers observed from April through July 2026, cross-occupation use rose from 13.1% to 25.9% of occupation-specific AI activity. ([OpenAI, Sep 16, 2026](https://openai.com/index/unlocking-new-ways-of-working/)) *Method: Vendor-reported, from work-related ChatGPT messages, April to July 2026.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's Global AI Diffusion report for Q2 2026 found that consumer Copilot users in the Global South have a 29.2% larger share of self-directed learning conversations and a 32.3% larger share of schoolwork and academic support than the global mix. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf)) *Method: Subset of consumer Copilot conversations, Q2 2026, relative to global mix, not absolute shares, authors warn the younger Global South population may drive it and results cover early adopters only.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that multimedia was the fastest-growing use case globally on individual ChatGPT plans in Q2 2026, accounting for 7.8% of messages. ([OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/)) *Method: Individual plans, Q2 2026, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Google's ATLAS reports that 14% of conversational (non-API) Gemini and AI Mode use is work-related and over 86% of interactions with conversational AI happen outside of work. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038); [OpenAI, Sep 2025](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)) *Method: Gemini App, AI Mode and API, Apr 6-19 2026, classifier-based, methods differ from OpenAI (June 2025 sample) and Anthropic (work/coursework/personal), so cross-lab shares are not directly comparable.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that among ChatGPT users who signed up between 2025-10-15 and 2026-05-01 (a 0.1% account sample), six months after signing up they sent 50% more messages per day than when they signed up. ([OpenAI, Jun 30, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-adoption-has-expanded/)) *Method: 0.1% sample of accounts created 2025-10-15 to 2026-05-01, activity to 2026-05-31, adults only, 53-category classifier, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic found the share of Claude conversations that are personal use rises from about 35% on weekdays to just under 50% on weekends, and tax-related conversations were eight times the May daily average on April 14, 2026. ([Anthropic, Jun 26, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)) *Method: Chat and Cowork, daily sample from the new telemetry, spring 2026, vendor-reported.*
- **Survey.** Pew found 49% of U.S. adults said in February 2026 that they ever use AI chatbots, compared with 33% in 2024, though the 2024 question was asked only of those who had heard about chatbots. About 42% of U.S. adults use chatbots to search for information, 20% for medical advice and 10% for emotional support, and 38% of employed adults use them for tasks at work. ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/)) *Method: Survey of 5,119 U.S. adults, Feb 17-23, 2026. Self-reported ever use. The 2024 question wording differed, so the rise from 33% has a wording break.*
- **Vendor-reported.** In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions, the share spent fixing broken code fell from 33% to 19% between October 2025 and April 2026, while operating software grew from 14% to 21%. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise)) *Method: Claude Code sessions Oct 2025 to Apr 2026, vendor-reported, model-classified work modes.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's Q1 2026 update says that within work-related usage on consumer plans, creating written and visual materials continued to lead but decreased over time, while more specialized tasks became more popular. ([OpenAI, May 11, 2026](https://openai.com/signals/research/2026q1-update/)) *Method: Individual plans, Q1 2026, the page states no numbers in text (charts only), vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index found 49% of classified Microsoft 365 Copilot chat goals support cognitive work such as analysis and problem-solving, with 19% working with people, 17% producing outputs and 15% finding information. ([Microsoft, May 5, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization)) *Method: Over 100,000 M365 Copilot chats, one week in February 2026, shares of classified user goals, not time, vendor-reported, enterprise product, so not comparable with consumer Copilot reports (methods differ).*
- **Vendor-reported.** By February 2026 Claude.ai coursework use fell from 19% to 12% and personal use rose from 35% to 42%, while the top 10 tasks fell from 24% to 19% of traffic; Anthropic attributes part of the coursework drop to student winter breaks. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai, 1M conversations, Feb 5-12 2026 vs November 2025, seasonal confound named by Anthropic (winter break, new casual signups from February).*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft AI's March 2026 analysis of over 500,000 January 2026 health conversations found mobile concentrates on personal health concerns while desktop is dominated by professional and academic work, within health queries only. ([Microsoft AI, Mar 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/copilot-health-usage-report.pdf)) *Method: Over 500,000 de-identified Copilot health conversations, January 2026, about 22% from the U.S., health subset only, so it does not generalize to all Copilot use.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's data show Claude.ai coding-related share fell from a peak of 40% in March 2025 to 34% in November 2025, and Educational Instruction and Library tasks rose from 9% (January 2025) to 15% (November 2025). ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai conversations by O-NET occupation group, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic classified Claude.ai conversations in November 2025 as 46% work, 19% coursework and 35% personal. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai, November 2025, new work/coursework/personal primitive.*
- **Survey.** Gallup found that what U.S. employees say they use AI for at work (42% consolidating information, 41% generating ideas, 36% learning new things in Q2 2025) did not change meaningfully from its first measure in Q2 2024. ([Gallup, Dec 14, 2025](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/699689/ai-use-at-work-rises.aspx)) *Method: U.S. employees who use AI at least yearly, Q2 2025 survey (May 7-16, 2025) vs Q2 2024, self-reported, counter-evidence to a fast change in work use cases.*
