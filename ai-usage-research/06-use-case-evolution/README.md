# Research Question 6: Use Case Evolution Over Time

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. The November 2026 section is current. Everything after it is the December 2025 compilation, kept as dated history. Superlatives and words like "latest", "leading" or "now" in that older text describe December 2025.*

- Anthropic's data show Claude.ai coding-related share fell from a peak of 40% in March 2025 to 34% in November 2025, and Educational Instruction and Library tasks rose from 9% (January 2025) to 15% (November 2025). ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- By February 2026 Claude.ai coursework use fell from 19% to 12% and personal use rose from 35% to 42%, while the top 10 tasks fell from 24% to 19% of traffic; Anthropic attributes part of the coursework drop to student winter breaks. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report))
- In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions, the share spent fixing broken code fell from 33% to 19% between October 2025 and April 2026, while operating software grew from 14% to 21%. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise))
- OpenAI's October 2026 release shows that across 74 countries the ten most common work activities fell from 56.0% of classified work messages in Q1 2025 to 51.1% in Q3 2026, so consumer ChatGPT work use became less concentrated. ([OpenAI, Oct 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-expanding-world-of-work/))
- Google's ATLAS reports that only 14% of conversational (non-API) Gemini and AI Mode use is work-related and over 86% of interactions happen outside work, versus about 30% work and 70% non-work in OpenAI's 2025 ChatGPT study. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038); [OpenAI, Sep 2025](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf))
- Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index found 49% of classified Microsoft 365 Copilot chat goals support cognitive work such as analysis and problem-solving, with 19% working with people, 17% producing outputs and 15% finding information. ([Microsoft, May 5, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 16 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.

## Primary Question
How are AI use cases evolving across platforms, and what does this reveal about user learning, platform maturation, and market trajectories?

## Key Hypothesis
AI use cases are broadening from technical early-adopter tasks toward mainstream personal and professional applications, with different platforms following different evolutionary paths.

## Significance
Understanding use case evolution is critical for:
- Predicting future AI application areas
- Platform positioning and feature development
- Identifying emerging market opportunities
- Understanding user sophistication progression
- Labor market impact forecasting

## Key Findings Summary
- **Decline in technical help**: ChatGPT technical help requests 12% → 5% (58% decline, July 2024 → July 2025) ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/))
- **Work → Personal shift**: ChatGPT non-work usage 53% → 72% (mid-2024 → mid-2025) ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: The paper's table gives 53% in June 2024 and 73% in June 2025 ([OpenAI paper, Sep 2025](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)).*
- **Education growth**: Claude educational tasks 9% → 12% (early 2025 → August 2025) ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report))
- **Scientific growth**: Claude scientific tasks 6% → 7% ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report))
- **Broadening beyond code**: Copilot fewer programming conversations, more culture/history (Jan → Sep 2025) ([Microsoft](https://microsoft.ai/news/its-about-time-the-copilot-usage-report-2025/))
- **Device-context shift**: Copilot desktop = work, mobile = health/wellness (different use cases by device) ([Microsoft](https://microsoft.ai/news/its-about-time-the-copilot-usage-report-2025/))
- **Automation rising**: Claude automation 27% → 39% (late 2024 → Aug 2025) ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report))
