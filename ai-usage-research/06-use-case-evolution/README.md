# Research Question 6: Use Case Evolution Over Time

## Primary Question
How are AI use cases evolving across platforms, and what does this reveal about user learning, platform maturation, and market trajectories?

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's data show Claude.ai coding-related share fell from a peak of 40% in March 2025 to 34% in November 2025, and Educational Instruction and Library tasks rose from 9% (January 2025) to 15% (November 2025). ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** By February 2026 Claude.ai coursework use fell from 19% to 12% and personal use rose from 35% to 42%, while the top 10 tasks fell from 24% to 19% of traffic; Anthropic attributes part of the coursework drop to student winter breaks. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions, the share spent fixing broken code fell from 33% to 19% between October 2025 and April 2026, while operating software grew from 14% to 21%. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise))
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's October 2026 release shows that across 74 countries the ten most common work activities fell from 56.0% of classified work messages in Q1 2025 to 51.1% in Q3 2026, so consumer ChatGPT work use became less concentrated. ([OpenAI, Oct 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-expanding-world-of-work/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Google's ATLAS reports that 14% of conversational (non-API) Gemini and AI Mode use is work-related and over 86% of interactions with conversational AI happen outside of work. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038); [OpenAI, Sep 2025](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf))
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index found 49% of classified Microsoft 365 Copilot chat goals support cognitive work such as analysis and problem-solving, with 19% working with people, 17% producing outputs and 15% finding information. ([Microsoft, May 5, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 16 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
