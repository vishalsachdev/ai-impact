# Research Question 4: Global Inequality in AI Access & Adoption

## Primary Question
How is AI adoption distributed globally, and is AI widening or narrowing global inequality gaps?

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft telemetry puts generative AI use at 28.8% of the working-age population in the Global North and 16.2% in the Global South in June 2026 (Q2), a 12.6 point gap, up from 10.6 points in H2 2025. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's March 2026 report (February 2026 Claude.ai data) says the top 20 countries account for 48% of population-adjusted usage, up from 45%, and that low-adoption countries fell slightly further behind. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's January 2026 report (November 2025 Claude.ai data) says cross-country usage per capita is strongly correlated with GDP and that there was no evidence of low-use countries catching up; a 1% rise in GDP per capita goes with 0.7% higher Claude usage per capita. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's India brief (November 2025 data) says India accounts for 5.8% of Claude.ai use, second to the U.S., but ranks 101st of 116 countries per capita. ([Anthropic, Feb 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/india-brief-economic-index))
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's Q2 2026 leaderboard is topped by the UAE (73.3%) and Singapore (64.3%), while the Global South average is 16.2%. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf))
- **Official source.** ITU estimates 2.2 billion people were offline in 2025, with 94% internet use in high-income countries versus 23% in low-income countries and 77% of men online versus 71% of women. ([ITU, Nov 17, 2025](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-11-17-facts-and-figures.aspx))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 13 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
