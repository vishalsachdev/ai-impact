# Global Inequality in AI: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft telemetry puts generative AI use at 28.8% of the working-age population in the Global North and 16.2% in the Global South in June 2026 (Q2), a 12.6 point gap, up from 10.6 points in H2 2025. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf)) *Method: Vendor-reported, aggregated Microsoft telemetry adjusted for OS and device share, internet penetration and population, measure is the share of people aged 15-64 who have used a generative AI product.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft finds that in its Copilot consumer sample, learning-related conversations are a larger share of use in the Global South: self-directed learning and skill development 29.2% larger than the global share and schoolwork and academic support 32.3% larger. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf)) *Method: Vendor-reported subset of consumer Copilot conversations, classified by a privacy-preserving intent classifier.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's Q2 2026 leaderboard is topped by the UAE (73.3%) and Singapore (64.3%), while the Global South average is 16.2%. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf)) *Method: Vendor-reported telemetry.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reported in June 2026 that lower-HDI countries had the fastest relative growth in weekly active ChatGPT users since July 2023, and in August 2026 that its Q2 2026 per-capita rankings show parts of Latin America, Africa and Oceania catching up. ([OpenAI, Jun 30, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-adoption-has-expanded/); [OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/)) *Method: Vendor-reported, individual plans (Free, Go, Plus, Pro), users 18+, relative growth against a July 2023 baseline, so it says nothing about absolute levels.*
- **Survey.** The Reuters Institute found weekly use of AI chatbots for news rose from 7% to 10% globally, driven largely by growth in parts of Asia, Africa and Latin America, and not at all in the USA, UK, France and Germany. ([Reuters Institute, Jun 16, 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/emerging-uses-ai-chatbots-news-and-what-it-means-journalism)) *Method: Online survey, 48 markets in the report, weekly use of chatbots for news only.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft's trend table shows the Global North at 22.9% (H1 2025), 24.7% (H2 2025) and 27.5% (Q1 2026), and the Global South at 13.1%, 14.1% and 15.4%, so the gap rose from 9.8 to 12.1 points; Microsoft says the North grew more than twice as fast as the South. ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, May 7, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q1.pdf))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's March 2026 report (February 2026 Claude.ai data) says the top 20 countries account for 48% of population-adjusted usage, up from 45%, and that low-adoption countries fell slightly further behind. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Vendor-reported, Claude.ai consumer conversations sampled in February 2026 compared with November 2025.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's India brief (November 2025 data) says India accounts for 5.8% of Claude.ai use, second to the U.S., but ranks 101st of 116 countries per capita. ([Anthropic, Feb 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/india-brief-economic-index)) *Method: Vendor-reported, Claude.ai conversations 2025-11-13 to 2025-11-20 (N = 975,160 globally), only countries with at least 200 observations are ranked.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's January 2026 report (November 2025 Claude.ai data) says cross-country usage per capita is strongly correlated with GDP and that there was no evidence of low-use countries catching up; a 1% rise in GDP per capita goes with 0.7% higher Claude usage per capita. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: Vendor-reported, sample window 2025-11-13 to 2025-11-20 for Claude.ai.*
- **Official source.** UNDP's December 2025 Asia-Pacific report says AI diffusion is measured in months and years rather than the decades of earlier technologies, and that a quarter of the region is offline. ([UNDP, Dec 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/undp-rbap-the-next-great-divergence.pdf)) *Method: The PDF itself shows 'December 2025'.*
- **Official source.** ITU estimates 2.2 billion people were offline in 2025, with 94% internet use in high-income countries versus 23% in low-income countries and 77% of men online versus 71% of women. ([ITU, Nov 17, 2025](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-11-17-facts-and-figures.aspx)) *Method: ITU estimates for 2025.*
- **Official source.** The World Bank's World Development Report 2026 says middle-income countries accounted for half of ChatGPT's global traffic within six months of launch, compared with about 80 years for the steam engine to reach lower-income countries. ([World Bank, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/wdr2026))
- **Expert compilation.** Stanford's 2026 AI Index economy chapter says generative AI adoption varies widely across countries and correlates strongly with GDP per capita, citing Singapore at 61%, the UAE at 64% and the U.S. ranked 24th at 28.3%. ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/economy))
