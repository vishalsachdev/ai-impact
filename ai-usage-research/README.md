# AI Platform Usage Research: Frontier Labs Analysis (2025-2026)

How people use AI platforms from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Microsoft, drawn from company reports, surveys and official statistics. For what AI can do, see **[AI Capabilities Research →](../ai-capabilities-research/)**

## November 2026 update

**Updated November 1, 2026.** The numbers on this page are current as of their dates.

### Share, by two different methods

Statcounter's October 2026 U.S. figures put ChatGPT at 37.1% and Gemini at 35.42% of tracked page views referred by chatbots ([Statcounter Global Stats, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share/all/united-states-of-america), third-party measurement). Similarweb reports ChatGPT's share of worldwide generative AI site visits fell from about 76% in June 2025 to about 53% by May 2026 ([Similarweb, Jul 29, 2026](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/), third-party measurement). Neither counts users, and the two are not comparable.

### What changed by research question

- **Automation:** augmentation led again in Anthropic's November 2025 sample, 52% to 45% ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report), vendor-reported).
- **Specialization:** enterprise is more than 40% of OpenAI's revenue ([OpenAI, Mar 31, 2026](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/), vendor-reported), and personal use reaches just under 50% of Claude conversations on weekends ([Anthropic, Jun 26, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report), vendor-reported).
- **Demographics:** 49% of U.S. adults use chatbots ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/), survey). The gender gap in ever-use has closed, but not in daily use ([Pew Research Center, Jun 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/the-gender-gap-in-ai/), survey).
- **Global inequality:** the North-South gap grew to 12.6 points in Microsoft's telemetry ([Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Sep 21, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Microsoft-AI-Diffusion-Report-2026-Q2.pdf), vendor-reported), and low-adoption countries fell slightly further behind on Claude ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report), vendor-reported). OpenAI reports faster relative growth in lower-HDI countries ([OpenAI, Jun 30, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-adoption-has-expanded/); [OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/), vendor-reported), so the measures do not agree.
- **Enterprise:** business adoption depends on the measure: 17% to 20% in the Census survey ([U.S. Census Bureau, May 26, 2026](https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html), official source), 55.9% of Ramp customers paying for AI ([Ramp, Oct 14, 2026](https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-oct-2026), third-party measurement), and nearly nine in ten McKinsey respondents ([McKinsey, Aug 25, 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai), survey).
- **Use cases:** Claude.ai's coding share fell from 40% to 34% ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report), vendor-reported). The ten most common work activities fell from 56.0% to 51.1% of consumer ChatGPT work messages ([OpenAI, Oct 26, 2026](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-expanding-world-of-work/), vendor-reported).
