# Platform Specialization: Detailed Analysis

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Alphabet's CEO remarks on 2026-10-28 say the Gemini app is now at one billion users, and do not restate whether the count is monthly. ([Google, Oct 28, 2026](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q3-2026/)) *Method: Company-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-6 launch post of 2026-10-07 says more than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week. ([OpenAI, Oct 7, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft said on 2026-07-29 it has over 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, with net seat adds more than doubling quarter over quarter. ([Microsoft, Jul 29, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4)) *Method: Company-reported.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Similarweb reports ChatGPT's share of worldwide generative AI website visits fell from about 76% in June 2025 to roughly 53% by May 2026, with Gemini rising from under 9% to about 27 to 28% and Claude from about 2% to close to 9%. ([Similarweb, Jul 29, 2026](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)) *Method: Similarweb worldwide web-traffic panel, share of visits to generative AI websites, not users and not app use.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Alphabet's CEO remarks on 2026-07-22 put the Gemini app at 950 million monthly active users, with daily active users tripling over the prior year. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/)) *Method: Company-reported on an earnings call.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's June 2026 Economic Index found the share of personal-use conversations in Claude chat and Cowork rises from around 35% on weekdays to just under 50% on weekends. ([Anthropic, Jun 26, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)) *Method: Anthropic-reported privacy-preserving telemetry sample, chat and Cowork conversations.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI Signals said on 2026-05-11 that during Q1 2026 the work versus non-work balance on consumer ChatGPT accounts continued in line with past trends, and gave no new percentage. ([OpenAI, May 11, 2026](https://openai.com/signals/research/2026q1-update/)) *Method: Vendor-reported, individual plans only (Free, Go, Plus, Pro).*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's March 2026 Economic Index found tasks tied to Computer and Mathematical occupations were 35% of Claude.ai conversations (February 2026 sample), while top-10 task concentration fell from 24% to 19%. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Anthropic-reported sample of 1 million conversations each from Claude.ai and first-party API, occupation-task mapping, so 'coding' here means Computer and Mathematical tasks, which may not equal the earlier '36% software development' label.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's March 2026 Economic Index says automation decreased sharply in the first-party API data (Appendix Figure A.3); the page gives no replacement percentage in the text. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Anthropic-reported, the page gives the direction (Appendix Figure A.3), not a replacement percentage in the sentence.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI stated on 2026-02-27 that ChatGPT has more than 900 million weekly active users and more than 50 million consumer subscribers. ([OpenAI, Feb 27, 2026](https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/)) *Method: Vendor-reported by OpenAI.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Ramp reported in February 2026 that about 79% of Anthropic's paying business customers also pay for OpenAI, and that both vendors had churn of around 4% of businesses per month. ([Ramp, Feb 11, 2026](https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-february-2026)) *Method: Ramp card and invoice spend data from Ramp's own customer base (not a random sample of US firms).*
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft said on 2026-01-28 it had 15 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, and over 450 million paid Microsoft 365 commercial seats in the same call. ([Microsoft, Jan 28, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q2))
- **Vendor-reported.** Microsoft said on 2026-01-28 that daily users of its consumer Copilot app increased nearly 3x year over year, and gave no absolute user count. ([Microsoft, Jan 28, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q2)) *Method: Company-reported growth rate only.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Statcounter's US AI chatbot share for October 2026 was ChatGPT 37.1%, Gemini 35.42%, Copilot 11.23%, Perplexity 10.34%, Claude 5.7%; this measures share of tracked page views referred by chatbots, not users. ([Statcounter Global Stats, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share/all/united-states-of-america)) *Method: Web-analytics page-view share on Statcounter's 1M+ member sites, page meta says 'based on over 3 billion monthly page views'.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Statcounter's worldwide AI chatbot share for October 2026 was ChatGPT 51.58%, Gemini 28.86%, Perplexity 15.04%, Claude 2.3%, Copilot 2.18% (share of referred page views, not users). ([Statcounter Global Stats, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share)) *Method: Same method and caveats as the US page, worldwide, all platforms.*
