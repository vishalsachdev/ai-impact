# Automation vs Augmentation: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's Enterprise Signals reports Codex generated 64% of combined Codex and ChatGPT output tokens among enterprise customers as of June 2026, and frontier firms (top 10% of usage) generate 8.3 times the output tokens per active user of typical firms, up from 2.6 times in January. ([OpenAI, Aug 12, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work/)) *Method: Vendor-reported enterprise telemetry, output tokens are a proxy for depth, and agentic workflows emit more tokens by design (OpenAI says so).*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that, across the globe, people are more than twice as likely to use ChatGPT for 'doing' at work than outside of work (individual accounts, Q2 2026). ([OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/)) *Method: OpenAI Signals, Free/Go/Plus/Pro individual accounts only, Q2 2026 country data, excludes Codex and enterprise, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Google's ATLAS study of about 15 million Gemini App, AI Mode and Gemini API interactions (April 6 to 19, 2026) found end-to-end automation under 10% of non-routine cognitive work conversations and use that is overwhelmingly collaborative. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038)) *Method: ArXiv preprint by Google authors, 14,653,926 interactions, Apr 6-19 2026, Workspace enterprise excluded, classifier-based and the authors call the intent split not definitive.*
- **Vendor-reported.** In Anthropic's linked survey of about 9,700 Claude users (usage mid-May to early June 2026), people who delegate most to Claude were the most optimistic about pay, job security and meaning, and heavier delegators reported learning at the same rate as others. ([Anthropic, Jun 26, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)) *Method: N about 9,700 linked respondents with 5+ sessions, survey not representative (about 30% computer and mathematical occupations, 12% women), self-reported, selection not ruled out.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic reports that AI autonomy is higher on Claude Code than on chat or Cowork for 26 of 31 output types, and that the average difference across all conversations is 0.37 points on a 1 to 5 scale. A blog-post task takes a median 13 chat or Cowork rounds but a single prompt in Claude Code. ([Anthropic, Jun 26, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)) *Method: Chat and Cowork vs Claude Code sessions, mid-2026 sample, model-judged autonomy scale, gap persists within the same model (Sonnet: 0.26 points).*
- **Vendor-reported.** In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions (October 2025 to April 2026), people made about 70% of the planning decisions but only about 20% of the execution decisions. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise)) *Method: About 400,000 interactive sessions from about 235,000 people, Oct 2025 to Apr 2026, Claude Code via CLI, Claude.ai and desktop, classifier-attributed decisions, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's Codex paper found Codex made up 99.8% of output tokens across Codex and ChatGPT among OpenAI workers, 63.3% among organizational users and 16.5% among individual users as of June 11, 2026. ([OpenAI, Jun 2026](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/5d1e1489-21c0-43e4-9d42-f87efdbf0082/the-shift-to-agentic-ai-evidence-from-codex.pdf)) *Method: Vendor-reported usage telemetry, OpenAI staff are unrepresentative by the authors' own statement.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's February 2026 sample (Feb 5 to 12, 1 million conversations from each of Claude.ai and the first-party API) showed augmentation up slightly on Claude.ai and automation down sharply in first-party API data, while the API continued to gain a higher share of Computer and Mathematical tasks. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai and 1P API, 1M conversations sampled from each, Feb 5-12 2026.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic found that Claude users with 6 or more months of tenure use Claude more collaboratively and have a 10% higher conversation success rate, and Anthropic says this contradicts its own 2025 guess that automated use is more typical of experienced users. ([Anthropic, Mar 24, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai, February 2026.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic measured users granting Claude Code more autonomy with experience: full auto-approve rose from roughly 20% of sessions for new users to over 40% for experienced users, and the 99.9th percentile turn length nearly doubled from under 25 to over 45 minutes between October 2025 and January 2026. ([Anthropic, Feb 18, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)) *Method: Interactive Claude Code sessions, users who signed up after 2025-09-19, vendor-reported, software engineering dominates the sample.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's November 2025 sample found augmentation back ahead of automation on Claude.ai (52% augmented, 45% automated), reversing the August 2025 result in which automation was ahead. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai conversations, November 2025 sample, Anthropic classifier, vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's directive share on Claude.ai fell 7 points to 32% in November 2025, from 39% in August 2025 (27% in January 2025). Anthropic says the August spike overstated how quickly automation was materializing, while the underlying trend is still toward greater automation. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: Claude.ai, directive mode only (one of the two automation modes).*
- **Vendor-reported.** In Anthropic's November 2025 sample, first-party API traffic was 64% directive and about three-quarters automation, against 32% directive and under half automation on Claude.ai. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)) *Method: 1P API vs Claude.ai, November 2025, the September 2025 report's 77% API automation figure was August 2025 data.*
