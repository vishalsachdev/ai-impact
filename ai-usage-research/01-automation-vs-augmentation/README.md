# Research Question 1: Automation vs Augmentation Patterns

## Primary Question
How are users interacting with AI platforms, as automation tools (directive task completion) versus augmentation tools (collaborative interaction)?

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's November 2025 sample found augmentation back ahead of automation on Claude.ai (52% augmented, 45% automated), reversing the August 2025 result in which automation was ahead. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's directive share on Claude.ai fell 7 points to 32% in November 2025, from 39% in August 2025 (27% in January 2025). Anthropic says the August spike overstated how quickly automation was materializing, while the underlying trend is still toward greater automation. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** In Anthropic's November 2025 sample, first-party API traffic was 64% directive and about three-quarters automation, against 32% directive and under half automation on Claude.ai. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- **Vendor-reported.** In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions (October 2025 to April 2026), people made about 70% of the planning decisions but only about 20% of the execution decisions. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise))
- **Vendor-reported.** Google's ATLAS study of about 15 million Gemini App, AI Mode and Gemini API interactions (April 6 to 19, 2026) found end-to-end automation under 10% of non-routine cognitive work conversations and use that is overwhelmingly collaborative. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038))
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that, across the globe, people are more than twice as likely to use ChatGPT for 'doing' at work than outside of work (individual accounts, Q2 2026). ([OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 13 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
