# Research Question 1: Automation vs Augmentation Patterns

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. The November 2026 section is current. Everything after it is the December 2025 compilation, kept as dated history. Superlatives and words like "latest", "leading" or "now" in that older text describe December 2025.*

- Anthropic's November 2025 sample found augmentation back ahead of automation on Claude.ai (52% augmented, 45% automated), reversing the August 2025 result in which automation was ahead. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- Anthropic's directive share (reported on this page in December 2025 as 27% to 39%) fell back from 39% in August 2025 to 32% in November 2025, and Anthropic says the August spike overstated how fast automation was growing, while the January 2025 to November 2025 direction still points to more automation. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- In Anthropic's November 2025 sample, first-party API traffic was 64% directive and about three-quarters automation, against 32% directive and under half automation on Claude.ai. ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report))
- In about 400,000 Claude Code sessions (October 2025 to April 2026), people made about 70% of the planning decisions but only about 20% of the execution decisions. ([Anthropic, Jun 16, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise))
- Google's ATLAS study of about 15 million Gemini App, AI Mode and Gemini API interactions (April 6 to 19, 2026) found end-to-end automation under 10% of non-routine cognitive work conversations and use that is overwhelmingly collaborative. ([Google, Jul 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00038))
- OpenAI reports that across countries people are more than twice as likely to use consumer ChatGPT to do a task or create something (doing) at work than outside work, where asking stays the largest category. ([OpenAI, Aug 6, 2026](https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/))

## Primary Question
How are users interacting with AI platforms—as automation tools (directive task completion) versus augmentation tools (collaborative interaction)?

## Key Hypothesis
There is a fundamental divergence emerging between how businesses deploy AI (automation) versus how individuals use AI (augmentation/advisory).

## Significance
Understanding this split is critical for:
- Predicting future AI development priorities
- Labor market impact assessment
- Educational curriculum design
- Policy interventions around AI deployment

## Key Findings Summary
- **Historic shift (August 2025)**: First time automation (39%) exceeded augmentation (61%) in Anthropic's data ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: The 39% is the directive share only, and 39% does not exceed 61%. Anthropic's report puts automation at about 49% against about 47% augmentation in August 2025 ([Anthropic, Sep 15, 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report)); augmentation led again in November 2025 at 52% against 45% ([Anthropic, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)).*
- **Business vs Consumer divide**: 77% of business API use is automation ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report)), while 73% of ChatGPT use is personal/advisory ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/)) *Corrected November 1, 2026: The 73% is the non-work share of sampled ChatGPT messages in June 2025. The Asking share, the closest match to advisory use, was 49% ([OpenAI paper, Sep 2025](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)).*
- **Platform specialization**: ChatGPT → personal advisor, Claude → work automation engine ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/09/15/openai-chatgpt-claude-anthropic-work-personal-use-new-data/))
- **Productivity gains**: 70% of Copilot users felt more productive, 29% faster at core tasks ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/copilots-earliest-users-teach-us-about-generative-ai-at-work))
