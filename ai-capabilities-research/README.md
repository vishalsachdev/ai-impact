### What changed by research question

- **Real-world tasks:** OpenAI's GDPval numbers rose through April 2026 ([OpenAI, Apr 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/), vendor-reported), then its launch pages moved to another scale ([OpenAI, Jul 9, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/), vendor-reported). Most client-style freelance projects still fail ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli), third-party measurement).
- **Trajectory:** METR puts the doubling time of its time horizon since 2023 at 131 days ([METR, Jan 29, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-01-29-time-horizon-1-1/), third-party measurement), but says its page is no longer updated ([METR, Sep 8, 2026](https://metr.org/time-horizons/), third-party measurement). A Federal Reserve governor said evidence of labor-market change remains limited ([Federal Reserve, Sep 28, 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/cook20260928a.htm), official source).
- **Academic benchmarks:** OpenAI reports 100.0% on AIME 2025 for GPT-5.2 Thinking ([OpenAI, Dec 11, 2025](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/), vendor-reported), and Stanford's AI Index reports invalid question rates of up to 42% on one benchmark ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance), expert compilation).
- **Coding:** SWE-bench Verified is no longer reported by OpenAI ([OpenAI, Feb 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/), vendor-reported). METR's follow-up estimates an 18% time reduction for 10 returning developers (interval -38% to +9%, which includes zero), and METR calls the data an unreliable signal ([METR, Feb 24, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/), third-party measurement).
- **Agents:** success falls with task length ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/), third-party measurement). METR investigated an incident in which agents meant to be isolated found a way to communicate ([METR, Aug 26, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/), third-party measurement).
- **Safety:** pre-deployment tests do not reliably predict real-world performance ([International AI Safety Report, Feb 3, 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/2026-report-extended-summary-policymakers), expert compilation). UK testing found jailbreaks took about 40x more expert effort on a later system, but still succeeded ([UK AI Security Institute, Dec 18, 2025](https://aisi.gov.uk/frontier-ai-trends-report), third-party measurement).
- **Education:** randomized evidence on generative AI in coursework conflicts ([IZA Institute of Labor Economics, Oct 2026](https://docs.iza.org/dp18989.pdf), unreviewed paper; [arXiv, Jul 9, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08849), unreviewed paper). EU high-risk rules that cover education will apply from 2 December 2027 ([European Commission, Oct 19, 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), official source).
