# Real-World Task Performance: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Google's Gemini 4 Argon model card (Oct 13, 2026) lists Agent's Last Exam pass rate at 39.5% for Argon, 34.2% for GPT-6 Astra and 38.2% for Claude Opus 5.5. OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra page reports 59.3% for Astra on a benchmark of the same name, and the source does not explain the gap. ([Google DeepMind, Oct 13, 2026](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/); [OpenAI, GPT-6 Astra page, undated](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic reports Claude Opus 5.5 at 1846 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1 at max effort, against 1735 for Fable 5.1 and 1708 for Opus 5; the same table lists GPT-6 Astra at 1542 and GPT-5.6 Sol at 1588 (Sep 22, 2026). ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)) *Method: Vendor-reported using Artificial Analysis's GDPval-AA v2.1.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's own Opus 5.5 launch page says benchmark margins at current capability levels are a less reliable guide to real-world differences, and that the gap between Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 is narrower in its own use than the scores suggest. ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)) *Method: Vendor statement, a counterweight to using single benchmark numbers in the cross-lab table.*
- **Vendor-reported.** From GPT-5.6 (Jul 9, 2026) OpenAI's launch table reports GDPval-AA v2, an Artificial Analysis Elo scale, instead of the GDPval win-or-tie rate: GPT-5.6 Sol 1,747.8, Claude Fable 5 1,759.6, GPT-5.5 1,493.7, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview 962.3. ([OpenAI, Jul 9, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-5.5 launch table reports GDPval wins-or-ties of 84.9% for GPT-5.5, 83.0% for GPT-5.4, 82.3% for GPT-5.5 Pro, 82.0% for GPT-5.4 Pro, 80.3% for Claude Opus 4.7 and 67.3% for Gemini 3.1 Pro (Apr 23, 2026); the OpenAI launch pages checked after this one report other benchmarks. ([OpenAI, Apr 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that GPT-5.4 matched or beat industry professionals on 83.0% of GDPval comparisons, up from 70.9% for GPT-5.2 (Mar 5, 2026). ([OpenAI, Mar 5, 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic reports that Claude Opus 4.6 scored about 144 Elo points above OpenAI's GPT-5.2 and 190 above Claude Opus 4.5 on GDPval-AA (Feb 5, 2026). ([Anthropic, Feb 5, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-6)) *Method: Vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that GPT-5.2 Thinking beat or tied industry professionals on 70.9% of GDPval comparisons judged by expert humans, against 38.8% for GPT-5 on the same measure (Dec 11, 2025). ([OpenAI, Dec 11, 2025](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Scale AI's Remote Labor Index (240 real freelance projects, 230 held private) lists Claude Opus 5.5 first at a 21.25% automation rate, with GPT 6 Astra at 20.83%, Fable 5.1 at 17.92% and Gemini Argon 4 at 17.08%. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)) *Method: Page is undated, it lists Gemini Argon 4 (released Oct 13, 2026) as 'NEW', so it was updated after that.*
- **Third-party measurement.** At launch the Remote Labor Index top agent (Manus) completed only 2.5% of the projects to a client-acceptable standard, which Scale describes as near zero; later rows (Opus 5.5 21.25%) show the rate rising but still below one in four. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)) *Method: Page undated.*
- **Expert compilation.** Stanford's 2026 AI Index reports that top models score 60 to 90% on evaluations in tax, mortgage processing, corporate finance and legal reasoning, with the top 15 models separated by as little as 3 percentage points. ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance)) *Method: Compilation of third-party benchmarks, not a new measurement, the page says 'high competency and reliability are required remain a great challenge'.*
