# Real-World Task Performance

## Research Question

**How well do AI models perform on authentic professional tasks across occupations?**

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-5.5 launch table reports GDPval wins-or-ties of 84.9% for GPT-5.5, 83.0% for GPT-5.4, 82.3% for GPT-5.5 Pro, 82.0% for GPT-5.4 Pro, 80.3% for Claude Opus 4.7 and 67.3% for Gemini 3.1 Pro (Apr 23, 2026); the OpenAI launch pages checked after this one report other benchmarks. ([OpenAI, Apr 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/))
- **Vendor-reported.** From GPT-5.6 (Jul 9, 2026) OpenAI's launch table reports GDPval-AA v2, an Artificial Analysis Elo scale, instead of the GDPval win-or-tie rate: GPT-5.6 Sol 1,747.8, Claude Fable 5 1,759.6, GPT-5.5 1,493.7, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview 962.3. ([OpenAI, Jul 9, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic reports Claude Opus 5.5 at 1846 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1 at max effort, against 1735 for Fable 5.1 and 1708 for Opus 5; the same table lists GPT-6 Astra at 1542 and GPT-5.6 Sol at 1588 (Sep 22, 2026). ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5))
- **Third-party measurement.** Scale AI's Remote Labor Index (240 real freelance projects, 230 held private) lists Claude Opus 5.5 first at a 21.25% automation rate, with GPT 6 Astra at 20.83%, Fable 5.1 at 17.92% and Gemini Argon 4 at 17.08%. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli))
- **Third-party measurement.** At launch the Remote Labor Index top agent (Manus) completed only 2.5% of the projects to a client-acceptable standard, which Scale describes as near zero; later rows (Opus 5.5 21.25%) show the rate rising but still below one in four. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://www.scale.com/leaderboard/rli))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's own Opus 5.5 launch page says benchmark margins at current capability levels are a less reliable guide to real-world differences, and that the gap between Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 is narrower in its own use than the scores suggest. ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 11 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
