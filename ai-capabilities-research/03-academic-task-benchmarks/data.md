# Academic Task Benchmarks: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Opus 5.5 table reports Humanity's Last Exam with tools at 67.7% for Opus 5.5, 65.6% for Fable 5.1, 63.6% for Opus 5 and 57.2% for GPT-6 Astra (Sep 22, 2026). Anthropic's Fable 5.1 page gives 65.0% for the same model. With-tools scores are not comparable to no-tools scores. ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)) *Method: Vendor-reported, with tools, max effort.*
- **Third-party measurement.** The Humanity's Last Exam owner site lists a Nature publication (Jan 28, 2026, Nature 649, 1139-1146) and the release of HLE-Diamond on Sept 22, 2026 as a migration path 'once frontier AI begins to hit the noise ceiling on the original HLE dataset'; its own leaderboard table still shows Gemini 3 Pro at 38.3 as top. ([CAIS and Scale AI, Sep 22, 2026](https://lastexam.ai)) *Method: Benchmark owner site. The leaderboard table on the page carries its own dataset date of April 3, 2025.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Fable 5.1 page reports Humanity's Last Exam at 60.9% without tools and 65.0% with tools for Claude Fable 5.1, against 57.8% and 63.8% for Fable 5 and 56.6% and 63.6% for Opus 5. ([Anthropic, Sep 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1)) *Method: Vendor-reported.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports GPT-5.2 Thinking at 34.5% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools and 45.5% with search and Python (Dec 11, 2025), up from 25.7% and 42.7% for GPT-5.1 Thinking. ([OpenAI, Dec 11, 2025](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports GPT-5.2 Thinking scoring 100.0% on AIME 2025 without tools (Dec 11, 2025), against 94.0% for GPT-5.1 Thinking. ([OpenAI, Dec 11, 2025](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Expert compilation.** Stanford's 2026 AI Index reports frontier models gained 30 percentage points in a single year on Humanity's Last Exam, and that a review found invalid question rates from 2% on MMLU Math to 42% on GSM8K. ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance)) *Method: Compilation.*
- **Third-party measurement.** OpenAI reports GPT-6 Astra at 96.0% on GPQA Diamond, and Epoch AI's independent run of GPT-6 Astra gives 0.96 (+/-0.01). ([Epoch AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://epoch.ai/data/benchmarks.csv); [OpenAI, GPT-6 Astra page, undated](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)) *Method: Independent run by Epoch at max effort, 96% with a standard error of about 1 point.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra page says Astra scored 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3 and 100% on ExploitBench. The page notes Astra was run with OpenAI's responses API harness, which changes two settings, and the scores are vendor-reported. ([OpenAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)) *Method: OpenAI-reported (vendor) result.*
- **Expert compilation.** Stanford's 2026 AI Index reports that Gemini Deep Think scored 35 points (gold) at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad, up from the 28-point silver in 2024, while the top model reads analog clocks correctly 50.6% of the time against 90.1% for humans. ([Stanford HAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance)) *Method: The IMO result is Google DeepMind's 2025 entry (DeepMind's own post dated 2025-07-21 says 'earning 35 out of a possible 42 points').*
