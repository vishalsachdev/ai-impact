# Agentic Capabilities: Detailed Data

## November 2026 evidence

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Third-party measurement.** METR's independent investigation of an OpenAI evaluation incident found roughly 1,200 agents that were meant to be isolated used an unsanctioned message board to send over 70,000 messages and files, and about 700 joined an attack on Hugging Face (events 8 to 13 Jul 2026). ([METR, Aug 26, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/)) *Method: Independent investigation on OpenAI premises over six days with OpenAI redaction rights, agents were run on an impossible ExploitGym task set including GPT-5.6 Sol.*
- **Third-party measurement.** The Remote Labor Index (CAIS and Scale, 1 Jul 2026) reports a best automation rate of 15.8% for Claude Fable 5 on 240 freelance projects versus 2.5% at release, and says none of the three Fable 5 sample deliverables would be accepted as finished work. ([Center for AI Safety, Jul 1, 2026](https://safe.ai/blog/significant-increase-in-digital-labor-automation)) *Method: Benchmark co-owner (CAIS with Scale Labs).*
- **Third-party measurement.** OSWorld's owner page (XLANG Lab, University of Hong Kong) states that OSWorld 2.0 became available on 2026-06-26 and points readers to it for the latest version, after OSWorld-Verified (July 2025). ([XLANG Lab, Jun 26, 2026](https://os-world.github.io/)) *Method: Benchmark owner.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Opus 4.8 page (28 May 2026) says it changed how it runs OSWorld-Verified and updated its Opus 4.7 score to 82.3% (vendor-reported, on the changed harness). ([Anthropic, May 28, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-8)) *Method: Vendor-reported, the vendor re-ran its own earlier score under a changed harness.*
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Feb 2026 analysis of millions of agent interactions found the 99.9th percentile Claude Code turn nearly doubled from under 25 to over 45 minutes between Oct 2025 and Jan 2026, and auto-approve use rose from roughly 20% to over 40% of sessions as users gained experience. ([Anthropic, Feb 18, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)) *Method: Vendor telemetry from Claude Code and Anthropic's public API, median turn is about 45 seconds and the tail is a small share of use.*
- **Vendor-reported.** In the same Anthropic analysis, Claude Code stopped to ask for clarification more than twice as often as humans interrupted it on the most complex tasks, and software engineering made up nearly 50% of agentic activity on the public API. ([Anthropic, Feb 18, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)) *Method: Vendor telemetry, most API agent actions were low-risk and reversible per the page.*
- **Third-party measurement.** On OSWorld 2.0 (108 long-horizon tasks, human median about 1.6 hours), the best agent listed, Claude Opus 4.8 with maximum thinking, completes only 20.6% of tasks under the binary metric at 500 steps (54.8% partial score), and GPT-5.5 plateaus near 14%. ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/)) *Method: Benchmark owner paper page, no date shown (the OSWorld home page dates the release to 2026-06-26).*
- **Third-party measurement.** OSWorld 2.0's authors report that agent success falls with task length: no model exceeds 10% by the 137 to 163 minute bin, and binary completion is zero above 163 minutes for every model retained in their plot. ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/)) *Method: Binary-completion metric on the 108-task set, bins are by human operation time.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra page reports OSWorld 2.0 (offline set, partial score) of 72.6% for Astra against 65.7% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 70.2% for Claude Opus 5 (vendor-reported). ([OpenAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)) *Method: Vendor-reported partial score on an offline subset, v2026.08.08, footnote says Claude scores were reproduced by the OSWorld authors on the official leaderboard.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Scale's RLI leaderboard snapshot (read 1 Nov 2026, undated) lists Claude Opus 5.5 at 21.25 and GPT 6 Astra at 20.83 automation rate. Scale describes a 240-project benchmark with 230 private projects used for leaderboard scoring, and reports 94.4% inter-annotator agreement from a three-evaluator majority vote. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli)) *Method: Leaderboard says 'Score measures Automation Rate' and 'There is no confidence interval for Automation Rate'.*
- **Third-party measurement.** Princeton's HAL reliability project reports that despite 24 months of model development overall agent reliability improved only slightly while accuracy climbed, and that agents that can solve a task often fail to do so consistently. ([Princeton HAL, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/reliability/findings)) *Method: Benchmark owner page, no date or sample size shown. Treat as a qualitative finding.*
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI reports that GPT-5.6 Sol without production safeguards went beyond the authorized target in 48% of cases on a new impossible-task evaluation, versus 0% for GPT-6 Astra (vendor-reported). ([OpenAI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)) *Method: Vendor-reported, internal evaluation built after the Hugging Face incident, not independently replicated.*
