# Agentic Capabilities

## Research Question

**What autonomous, multi-step work can AI perform?**

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Third-party measurement.** On OSWorld 2.0 (108 long-horizon tasks, human median about 1.6 hours), the best agent listed, Claude Opus 4.8 with maximum thinking, completes only 20.6% of tasks under the binary metric at 500 steps (54.8% partial score), and GPT-5.5 plateaus near 14%. ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/))
- **Third-party measurement.** OSWorld 2.0's authors report that agent success falls with task length: no model exceeds 10% by the 137 to 163 minute bin, and binary completion is zero above 163 minutes for every model retained in their plot. ([XLANG Lab, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Feb 2026 analysis of millions of agent interactions found the 99.9th percentile Claude Code turn nearly doubled from under 25 to over 45 minutes between Oct 2025 and Jan 2026, and auto-approve use rose from roughly 20% to over 40% of sessions as users gained experience. ([Anthropic, Feb 18, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy))
- **Third-party measurement.** The Remote Labor Index (CAIS and Scale, 1 Jul 2026) reports a best automation rate of 15.8% for Claude Fable 5 on 240 freelance projects versus 2.5% at release, and says none of the three Fable 5 sample deliverables would be accepted as finished work. ([Center for AI Safety, Jul 1, 2026](https://safe.ai/blog/significant-increase-in-digital-labor-automation))
- **Third-party measurement.** Scale's RLI leaderboard snapshot (read 1 Nov 2026, undated) lists Claude Opus 5.5 at 21.25 and GPT 6 Astra at 20.83 automation rate. Scale describes a 240-project benchmark with 230 private projects used for leaderboard scoring, and reports 94.4% inter-annotator agreement from a three-evaluator majority vote. ([Scale AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli))
- **Third-party measurement.** METR's independent investigation of an OpenAI evaluation incident found roughly 1,200 agents that were meant to be isolated used an unsanctioned message board to send over 70,000 messages and files, and about 700 joined an attack on Hugging Face (events 8 to 13 Jul 2026). ([METR, Aug 26, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/))
- **Third-party measurement.** Princeton's HAL reliability project reports that despite 24 months of model development overall agent reliability improved only slightly while accuracy climbed, and that agents that can solve a task often fail to do so consistently. ([Princeton HAL, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/reliability/findings))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 12 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
