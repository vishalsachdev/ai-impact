# Capability Trajectory & Economic Impact

## Research Question

**How fast are AI capabilities improving, and what are the projected economic impacts?**

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Third-party measurement.** METR's Time Horizon 1.1 post (Jan 29, 2026) reports that the doubling time of the 50% time horizon since 2023 is 131 days on the new task suite versus 165 days on the original, and about 196 days (7 months) across 2019-2025. ([METR, Jan 29, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-01-29-time-horizon-1-1/))
- **Third-party measurement.** METR states its time-horizon page is no longer actively updated (last updated Sept 8, 2026), and its update log lists no model after Claude Mythos Preview (early), added May 8, 2026. ([METR, Sep 8, 2026](https://metr.org/time-horizons/))
- **Third-party measurement.** Epoch AI released a major FrontierMath update on 2026-06-12 that addressed errors in 42% of problems. The v2 dataset has 338 problems: 295 in Tiers 1-3 and 43 in Tier 4. ([Epoch AI, Jun 12, 2026](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath))
- **Third-party measurement.** Epoch AI's own runs on the private FrontierMath Tier 1-3 (v2) set give Claude Opus 5.5 91.2%, GPT-6 Astra 93.7% and GPT-6.1 Sol 93.7%. OpenAI reported 40.3% for GPT-5.2 in December 2025 on the earlier set. The two versions differ, so these scores are not like for like. ([Epoch AI, undated page, read Nov 1, 2026](https://epoch.ai/data/benchmarks.csv); [OpenAI, Dec 11, 2025](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/))
- **Consultancy estimate.** McKinsey's AI value curve (Oct 14, 2026) estimates a roughly $2.5 trillion annual global opportunity, $2.52 trillion, from deploying AI on tasks where it is cheaper than current work, and says a 30% rise in AI cost would cut the global pool from $2.52 trillion to $1.67 trillion. ([McKinsey, Oct 14, 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-ai-value-curve-the-economics-of-applying-ai-to-existing-work))
- **Official source.** The Dallas Fed (Jul 7, 2026) reports U.S. labor productivity grew at a 2.4 percent annualized rate since the start of 2024 against 1.6 percent in the five pre-pandemic years, and 3.7 percent in the three most AI-exposed sectors against 1.7 percent in the rest of the economy. ([Dallas Fed, Jul 7, 2026](https://www.dallasfed.org/research/economics/2026/0707))
- **Official source.** Federal Reserve Governor Cook said on Sept 28, 2026 that there is limited evidence that AI is changing the structure of the labor market so far, with unemployment and layoffs low and flat over two years, and that AI-related demand is adding some inflation pressure. ([Federal Reserve, Sep 28, 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/cook20260928a.htm))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 19 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
