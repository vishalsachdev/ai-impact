# Coding and Research Capabilities

## Research Question

**What can AI do in software development and research contexts?**

## November 2026 update

*Updated November 1, 2026. Evidence through October 2026. Each item carries an evidence label, explained on the home page.*

- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI audited 138 SWE-bench Verified problems that o3 did not consistently solve and found that 59.4% had material test-design or problem-description flaws, and said it has stopped reporting SWE-bench Verified scores. ([OpenAI, Feb 23, 2026](https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/))
- **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI's GPT-5.6 launch table (9 Jul 2026) lists SWE-Bench Pro at 64.6% for GPT-5.6 Sol, 80.3% for Claude Mythos 5, 80% for Claude Fable 5 and 69.2% for Claude Opus 4.8 (vendor-reported, harnesses may differ). ([OpenAI, Jul 9, 2026](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 page (22 Sep 2026) reports Terminal-Bench 4.0 and FrontierCode v1.1 results. It says Opus 5.5 at default effort scores 54.6% on FrontierCode, beating GPT-6 Astra's top score of 53.3%. ([Anthropic, Sep 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5))
- **Third-party measurement.** METR's Feb 2026 follow-up (57 developers, 800+ tasks, run from Aug 2025) estimates a time change of -18% (interval -38% to +9%, so it includes zero) for 10 returning developers and -4% (interval -15% to +9%) for 47 new developers, against +19% in the early-2025 study. METR calls the data an unreliable signal because of selection effects. ([METR, Feb 24, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/))
- **Third-party measurement.** METR's May 2026 survey of 349 technical workers (Feb to Apr 2026) found a median self-reported 1.4 to 2x change in the value of work from AI tools and a 3x median self-reported speed change, with METR warning that survey self-reports may overstate. ([METR, May 11, 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-11-ai-usage-survey/))
- **Vendor-reported.** Anthropic's Institute reports that as of May 2026 more than 80% of the code merged into Anthropic's codebase was authored by Claude (vendor-reported), and that the typical engineer merged 8 times as much code per day in Q2 2026 as in 2024. ([Anthropic, Jun 4, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement))
- **Vendor-reported.** At Google Cloud Next (22 Apr 2026) Sundar Pichai said 75% of all new code at Google is AI-generated and approved by engineers, up from 50% the previous fall (vendor-reported). ([Google, Apr 22, 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/cloud-next-2026-sundar-pichai/))
- **Peer-reviewed.** Sakana AI's The AI Scientist was published in Nature on 25 Mar 2026: one of three AI-generated manuscripts submitted to an ICLR workshop (acceptance rate 70%) averaged 6.33 from reviewers (6, 7, 6), the other two did not meet the bar, and the paper was withdrawn under a pre-set protocol. ([Nature, Mar 25, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10265-5))

Vendor-reported numbers come from the company named and were not independently checked unless stated. All 23 new findings, with methods, are in **[the November 2026 evidence](./data.md#november-2026-evidence)**.
